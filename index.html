About TGPU Gulf Advisory

Built between Malaysia and the Arab world.

TGPU Gulf Advisory is a Malaysian business focused on practical cross-border business development and GCC market-entry coordination.

Our founder, Muhammad Mamduh Bin Saffin, has academic and professional experience spanning Malaysia and Saudi Arabia. His professional background includes multinational corporate exposure and direct experience supporting Malaysian businesses exploring GCC e-commerce and market-entry opportunities.
